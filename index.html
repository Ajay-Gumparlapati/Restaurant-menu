Services

Food and Beverage Services

  • Dine-in Service: Meals served to customers at tables in the restaurant.
  • Takeaway/To-go: Customers order food and pick it up to eat elsewhere.
  • Delivery Service: Food is delivered to the customer's location.
  • Catering: Providing food services for events, parties, or corporate functions.
  • Drive-thru Service: Common in fast food establishments for quick pickup.

Customer Service

  • Table Service: Waitstaff take orders, serve food, and handle payment.
  • Self-Service: Customers order and collect their food themselves (e.g., buffets).
  • Online Ordering Support: For delivery or takeaway, through website/apps.
  • Reservation Management: Booking tables in advance, either online or by phone.

Ambience and Experience

  • Interior Design & Cleanliness: Comfortable seating, decor, and hygienic space.
  • Music/Entertainment: Live music, TVs, or themed events.
  • Wi-Fi and Charging Stations: Especially in cafes or casual dining spots.
